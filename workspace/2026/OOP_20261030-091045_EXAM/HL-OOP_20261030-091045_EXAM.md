## 1. Vision 🔒 FROZEN

A student chooses their name, reads clear exam rules and completes a 60-minute OOPoman exam covering all three course modules. The result explains the score and every failed practical criterion; a score of at least 30/40 produces a local certificate recognizing successful completion of the course, without claiming verified identity or authenticity.

**Impact:** Students can demonstrate what they learned and see how their work met the course criteria, while their exam record remains in their browser and their existing course progress stays separate.

> **Explicitly imagined stakeholder quote:** “Я вижу, за что получил каждый балл, что ещё стоит разобрать и почему мой результат означает успешную сдачу курса.”

## 3. Target State (To-Be) 🔒 FROZEN

Add one exam experience to OOPoman with exactly 20 single-answer multiple-choice questions and exactly three executable practical tasks: лёгкая, средняя and продвинутая. It covers all three modules, treats OOP as the main subject, runs practical work in the course's existing terminal once that runtime and its boundaries are ready, and compares student code with explicit criteria and expected results.

The exam has a maximum of 40 points and passes at 30 points or higher. The owner-approved distribution is 1 point per test question, split 8/7/5 across Modules 1/2/3, and 4/6/10 points for the лёгкая/средняя/продвинутая practical tasks. Each practical point must map to a discrete, visible criterion. A syntax/runtime error or unmet criterion displays the concrete failure and awards no point for that criterion. Partial credit is limited to separately listed criteria that pass. A task remains incomplete whenever any mandatory criterion fails, even if other criteria earn partial points.

Before starting, show the rules, state that no materials are allowed, and show the structure, 60-minute limit, maximum and pass score. The timer begins only after “Начать экзамен”; at timeout, auto-submit the current answers and finish the attempt. The student chooses one of exactly three names: Мухитов Арсений, Куандыков Жасулан, Саржан Аубакиров. Do not offer free-text name entry.

The result shows the chosen name, score out of 40, pass/fail status, test and practical breakdown, and failed criteria. After a pass, show the owner-approved message: “Поздравляем, [Имя]! Вы успешно сдали экзамен и завершили курс OOPoman. Результат: [баллы]/40.” Then offer a 1–5 star course rating. The local certificate states successful completion of the course, while disclosing that this static site does not verify identity or certificate authenticity. The exam record and rating use a separate local browser key, proposed as `oopoman.exam.v1`; they are not sent to an external service and do not alter `oopoman.progress.v1`.

A separate teacher key with correct answers, rationales, practical solutions and rubrics stays outside `course/` and is excluded from `dist/`. Because the grader runs in the browser, students can inspect the questions, correct-answer behavior and practical checks. Neither the UI nor the certificate may claim anti-cheating protection, secret client-side answers, authenticated identity or independent verification.

The exam stays unavailable on a public build until all three modules are available to students and the terminal dependency has an agreed route/readiness for the required modules. Do not add a parallel runner or change the active terminal task's common site files before that agreement.

## 6. Definition of Failure (DoF) 🔒 FROZEN

- ❌ 1. A question has multiple defensible answers, a cue reveals the answer, or it tests a topic not in the completed course material.
- ❌ 2. The required existing terminal is unavailable or its boundary/readiness for the required modules is not established; do not create a second runner to bypass the dependency.
- ❌ 3. The task cannot show a concrete criterion failure or expected result, or a failed mandatory criterion is shown as a fully solved task.
- ❌ 4. Exam state leaks into course scores/progress/completion, or any result/rating is transmitted externally without a new owner-approved scope.
- ❌ 5. The answer key file enters `dist/` or browser-loaded resources, or product text claims answer secrecy, identity verification or certificate authenticity that the static client cannot provide.
- ❌ 6. Module 3 remains unavailable to the intended students when the exam is exposed to them.

**On failure:** stop the affected scope, preserve current course state and exam data, record the exact criterion and observable failure in the Executor result, and return for the owner decision or the existing RT dependency route. Do not claim a pass or complete the task until the failed condition is resolved.

## 7. Principles 🔒 FROZEN

1. **Course fidelity** — test only concepts and practical criteria actually taught in the three modules and their labs.
2. **Observable grading** — every point follows a visible question key or an atomic practical criterion with an expected outcome.
3. **Honest result** — distinguish exam score, task completeness and a local certificate; do not imply authentication or anti-cheating.
4. **Separate state** — exam records and ratings cannot change course progress, points or completion.
5. **One runtime** — reuse the existing course terminal only within its approved and returned boundary; never build a parallel runner.
6. **Clear student control** — explain rules before timing, allow only the approved names, and show specific feedback after submission.
